Welcome to the Oceans of NYC blog.

We've been quietly tracking every Fisker Ocean operating in New York City since late 2025, and we figured it was time to have a place to share updates beyond the sighting feed itself.

## What you'll find here

This blog will be home to:

- **Milestone posts** — when we cross 100 sightings, 200 Oceans found, a new contributor record, or any other number worth marking
- **Community spotlights** — stories from contributors who've been out there finding these vehicles
- **Project updates** — new features, data improvements, and changes to how the site works
- **Observations** — things we notice in the data, patterns in where Oceans get spotted, that kind of thing

## The project so far

What started as a curiosity has turned into something genuinely community-driven. Contributors across all five boroughs are out there finding these vehicles, and the dataset has grown into a real record of the Fisker Ocean's presence in NYC taxi and rideshare fleets.
