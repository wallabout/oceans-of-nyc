> Two people. One Ocean. Same day. Neither of them knows about the other.

That's a missed connection.

We've added a [Missed Connections page](/missed-connections) that finds every instance where two different contributors photographed the same vehicle on the same day. Each row shows both sightings side by side — contributor, borough, time — with a gap column showing how close in time they were.

Some of these are minutes apart. Same car, different blocks, different people. The closest ones are a little eerie.

The table is sortable by gap or by date, and you can filter to any contributor to see their missed connections specifically.

Check it out at [oceansofnyc.com/missed-connections](/missed-connections).
