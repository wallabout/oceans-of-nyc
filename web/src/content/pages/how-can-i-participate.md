The participation bar is intentionally low: if you can take a photo, you can play.

## What to do

1. **Spot an Ocean.** They look like a Tesla Model Y crossed with a stealth bomber. Most of them have a plate starting with `T` and ending with `C` — that's the NYC for-hire vehicle pattern.
2. **Take a photo with the license plate visible.** GPS coordinates from the photo are how we place the sighting on the map, so leave location data on. Most phones do this by default.
3. **Send it in.** The [submit page](/submit) has the details — currently we accept submissions via SMS/MMS.

That's it. We do the rest: extracting GPS, validating the plate against the NYC TLC database, reverse-geocoding to a neighborhood, posting the sighting to Bluesky with attribution.

## What counts

- A photo where the plate is legible
- Taken in NYC (or anywhere, really — there's a [Road Tripper badge](/p/what-are-badges) for out-of-town sightings)
- Of an actual Fisker Ocean, not a Polestar or a Mustang Mach-E or wishful thinking

## What you get

- Attribution on the public sighting record
- [Ocean Points](/p/ocean-points) for first sightings
- [Badges](/badges) — badges for various milestones
- The dignity of having contributed to a serious work of automotive scholarship
