We've added **Tags** so the good photos can find their way to the top.

[Badges](/p/what-are-badges) are earned by contributors and calculated automatically from their sightings. Tags are the opposite: they're nominations from whoever is looking at a photo. See a coffee-colored Ocean, or two of them in one frame, or a shot that just belongs in a coffee table book? Say so.

There's no account and no sign-up. Click the tag, and it counts.

## The tags

- 🟥 **Rare Color: Red** — one of the rare red Oceans
- 🟫 **Rare Color: Coffee** — one of the rare coffee-colored Oceans
- 👯 **Multi-Ocean** — two or more Oceans in a single frame
- 🌴 **CA Mode** — the rear window is down, the way California Mode leaves it
- 📸 **Great Photography** — a photo that belongs in a coffee table book
- 🗽 **That's So NYC** — a photo that captures New York City perfectly

There's also a 🚩 **Report** tag for a photo that's broken or isn't of the right vehicle. That one never shows up as a chip on the photo — a single visitor shouldn't be able to publicly label someone's submission as broken — but we see it and can act on it.

## Where to tag

Every card in the [feed](/feed) has a **Tag photo** button that opens the picker.

If you'd rather just work through the archive, [/random](/random) serves one photo at a time and leans toward photos nobody has tagged yet, so the older sightings get looked at too. It's the fastest way to help, and it's a little addictive.

The [tags page](/tagged) is the payoff: every photo that has earned a tag, filterable by tag and sortable by how many nominations it has. Sort by **Most tagged** with **Great Photography** selected and you've got the project's greatest hits.

Right now nearly every photo in the catalog has never been tagged. Go [tag some](/random).
