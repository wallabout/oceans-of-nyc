On May 6, 2026, [@obtusatum.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/obtusatum.bsky.social/post/3ml7tlnznas2d) submitted what we believe is the first **Ocean-ception** sighting: T103516C, photographed from inside T800756C.

A Fisker Ocean, taken in an Uber that was itself a Fisker Ocean.

We hadn't anticipated this when designing the project, but in retrospect it was inevitable. Most Oceans on NYC roads are TLC for-hire vehicles. If you ride enough Ubers in NYC, you will eventually be inside one. From there, it's just a matter of looking out the window at the right traffic light.

No new badge for this yet. We're thinking about it.
