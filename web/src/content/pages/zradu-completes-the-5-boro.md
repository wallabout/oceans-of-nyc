[Zach R](https://bsky.app/profile/zradu1.bsky.social) earned the **5 Boro** badge — a sighting logged in each of Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

Of all the [badges](/badges) in the catalog, 5 Boro is probably the most quietly punishing. Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Queens fall out of normal life if you're paying attention. The Bronx takes a bit of intention. Staten Island takes a ferry, a bridge, or a strong commitment to the bit.

Zradu has been one of the project's most active contributors and is currently a top-10 ranked spotter on the [stats page](/stats). The 5 Boro badge is well-earned.

If you're chasing it too: the [Staten Island sightings post](/p/first-sightings-in-staten-island) has the only known precedent for that borough. Good luck.
