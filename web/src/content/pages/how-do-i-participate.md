The participation bar is intentionally low: if you can take a photo, you can play. Spot an Ocean, get the license plate in frame, and send it in. We do the rest — extracting GPS, validating the plate against the NYC TLC database, reverse-geocoding to a neighborhood, and [posting the sighting to Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/oceansofnyc.com) with your attribution.

There are two ways to submit, and they're equally good. Pick whichever is faster in the moment.

## Submit by SMS

This is the fastest way when you're out on the street. Take a picture with the plate visible and text it to:

**623-263-4638** — that's **OCEAN-FIND-8** on your keypad.

From there it's a quick back-and-forth:

1. **Send the photo.** We pull the GPS coordinates from the image, so leave location data on — most phones keep it on by default. We'll ask for the plate.
2. **Reply with the plate.** Either the full `T######C` format or just the 6 digits. We validate it against the TLC database and tell you which Ocean you found.
3. **Confirm.** Reply `YES` and the sighting is logged.

A few commands are handy mid-conversation:

- `HELP` — show the help message
- `CANCEL` — abandon the current submission
- `SKIP` — skip setting a contributor name

## Submit by web

No phone texting? Use the [**Submit** page](/submit) (also in the top nav). Upload your photo, enter the plate and borough, and add the name you'd like to be credited under. There's an optional email field — using the same email each time keeps all your sightings tied to one contributor, so your stats and [badges](/badges) stay together.

Either way, once it's in you'll see where your sighting landed: which number it was for that vehicle, how many Oceans have been found overall, and any new badges you earned.

## Follow along on Bluesky

Every sighting gets posted publicly, so the liveliest way to follow the hunt is on Bluesky:

**[@oceansofnyc.com](https://bsky.app/profile/oceansofnyc.com)**

New badges, milestones (we track [percentage of the fleet found](/p/fifty-percent)), and the occasional [Ocean-in-the-wild oddity](/p/ocean-in-a-carwash) all show up there first. It's also the easiest place to ask a question — the account is run by real people.

## Find your contributor profile page

Every contributor gets a public profile at `/contributor/<your-id>` showing your sightings, first-finds, [Ocean Points](/p/ocean-points) (◎p), and [badges](/badges).

The easiest way to find yours: click your name anywhere it appears on the site. Your name is a link on:

- the [Feed](/feed), on each sighting card
- the [Stats](/stats) leaderboard
- any individual Ocean's page, next to sightings you logged

Any of those clicks lands on your profile. Bookmark it — it's the running record of everything you've spotted. (Submitting with the same email, or setting a Bluesky handle, keeps every sighting attached to that one profile.)

## What counts

- A photo where the plate is legible
- Taken in NYC — or anywhere, really; there's a [Road Tripper badge](/p/what-are-badges) for out-of-town sightings
- Of an actual Fisker Ocean, not a Polestar or a Mustang Mach-E or wishful thinking

That's the whole thing. Go find some Oceans.
