May 8, 2026 was a big day at Oceans of NYC. Several records fell at once:

- **First 100+ sighting day** — more sightings logged in 24 hours than ever before.
- **21 contributors** in a single day — also a record.
- **3,000 total sightings** crossed.
- **1,500 unique Oceans** spotted.
- **60.0%** of the known fleet cataloged.
